TROUBLESHOOTING

If something isn't working

❌ "Cannot reach backend"
Free tier is sleeping. Wait 30 seconds → tap TEST CONNECTION again. Normal on first use each day.
⚠️ Forex shows WAIT / "API key not set"
You forgot to add TWELVEDATA_API_KEY in Render env vars. Go to Render → your service → Environment → add it there.
⚠️ Only BTC/ETH works, forex doesn't
Same as above — TwelveData key is missing. BTC/ETH use Binance (no key needed). Forex needs the key.
ℹ️ Signal says "30 candles loading"
Backend just woke up. Tap GENERATE again after 20 seconds. Data is downloading from TwelveData.
ℹ️ 🚨 BLOCK appears instead of signal
Payout is below 85% on PocketOption. The app is protecting you — don't trade on that asset right now. Switch to another asset or wait.
